ONE-WAY TRIP TO EARTH, 1980

Machines from the runaway star NGC-1980 have come ashore in a 1980 archipelago with weapons from our future.

HOW TO PLAY · CONTROLS

1980. Machines from the runaway star NGC-1980 have jumped back in time, one way, and come ashore in a remote archipelago — with their own war machines and modern weapons from our future. A joint Cold War task force is sent to stop them. THE ISLAND WAR — your carrier ALBATROSS against the enemy carrier KRAKEN. Islands give fuel (F), materiel (M) or radar sight (R) to whoever holds them. You only see what your helicopter, ships and islands can see.

TAKE ISLANDS: knock out their AA guns, then land on the pad and hold it — or send a CH-46 SEA KNIGHT with a squad of Marines. Cave fortresses fall when you steal the pod from their cave. Sink the KRAKEN or take its headquarters IRONHOLD to win; lose the ALBATROSS and the war is lost.

The enemy's factories are building the LEVIATHAN, a railgun monitor — bomb the factories and its shipyard to hold it back. Their islands ship their output home in autonomous CONTAINER SHIPS: sink them to cut the supply.

M command map: click the sea to sail the carrier there, click an island to select it · 1 SEA KNIGHT + 13 Marines · 2 AA gun on our island · 3 new helicopter · 4 Tomcat escort (top cover) · 6 SKY CARRIER refit (Shift+6 or F6: at once, free): the ALBATROSS gets lift fans and flies — land on her and press Tab to take the helm on the bridge (W/S telegraph, A/D wheel, Space/Ctrl lift, V outside, click + mouse to look round; she is slow and heavy). Bombs land on the yellow ring. Land on the carrier or any friendly pad to refuel, repair and rearm.

YOU COMMAND THE TASK FORCE  \ command board: take out any unit at the ALBATROSS or a friendly island — Huey, Hornet, Tomcat, M60A3, M113A2, the motor pool, a Marine — or switch to one already out (/ next unit). The unit you leave carries on: crews fly it home, the wingman flies the jet, vehicles park. Your lives are the fleet's reserves: the war is lost when the fleet is spent.

BOTH SIDES ARE MATCHED  Our outposts ship their output to the ALBATROSS in MSC freighters, as theirs do in container ships — guard the convoys, the WASPs hunt them. Shift+1-5 (map or command board, at the selected island): 1 AH-1T COBRA sortie (their WASPs' match), 2 an M60A3 by LCM-8 that fights on its own (their seized MBTs'), 3 E-2C HAWKEYE (their REAPER's: she finds the KRAKEN), 4 BGM-109 salvo from our islands (their SHAHEDs'), 5 task the battleship USS IOWA (their LEVIATHAN's: our factories reactivate her; bombard / escort / hunt). New AA orders on our HQ islands put up MIM-23 HAWK batteries (their LANCE's). And the RUNAWAY can refit the KRAKEN with lift fans: she flies too.

RESEARCH  Shift+\ (or T on the command board): the task force sails with Hueys, Marines, Sea Knights, AA guns, the motor pool and Hornets. Tomcats, LCM-8 armour (M113A2, M60A3), C-130 logistics (L), field medicine, radar pickets, Seabees, island production and the SKY CARRIER are researched with materiel, one programme at a time, on war time: ENTER starts, X cancels (refunded). Locked orders say what they need. The RUNAWAY research too — the feed tells you what they field.

DOWNED? Set down on land and press L: home base sends the dropship MULE 1 with a para repair team, from over the horizon. You fly it from the flight deck — same controls as the helicopter (stick / W S A D, Q E rudder, collective keys = throttle); H autopilot, V outside view. Jump with F on the green light (above 40 m, below 140 kt) — the green smoke shows the wind. Then steer the canopies (A/D, S brakes). Stuck for good? Esc → respawn on the carrier.
